CBD and CBG,explained

Two cannabinoids from the same plant. Neither gets you high. Here’s how they differ, and what the research actually shows so far.

The Bliss Brief5 min readLast checked October 23, 2026

← The Bliss Brief

Hemp is a variety of cannabis that is naturally low in THC, the compound that gets you high. It also makes dozens of other compounds called cannabinoids. Two of them, CBD and CBG, are what go into every bottle of Altrd Bliss.

CBDCannabidiol · The chill older sibling

Smooths the edges, eases tension, and helps you drift into deeper sleep.

CBGCannabigerol · The focused cousin

Sharpens your thinking, clears the fog, and keeps your body in that easy, steady flow.

Same plant, different jobs

Both act on the endocannabinoid system, a network of receptors throughout the body that helps regulate mood, stress and sleep. CBD is the better studied of the two. It’s the active ingredient in Epidiolex, a prescription medicine the FDA approved in 2018 for certain seizure disorders. CBG has far less research behind it, but that is starting to change.

“Feeling good shouldn’t mean giving up the ritual, flavor, or fun of a great drink.”Erika Isaac, co-founder, in Voyage LA

What the research says about CBG

In 2024, researchers ran a placebo-controlled trial on CBG, anxiety and stress. It was small and early, and it’s worth reading for exactly that reason.

The study

Acute effects of cannabigerol on anxiety, stress and mood

Published
Scientific Reports, 2024
Who
34 healthy adults, each tested once with 20 mg of CBG and once with a placebo
Found
Lower self-rated anxiety and stress with CBG, a small possible memory boost, and no intoxication or impairment
Caveats
A small sample, a single dose and funding from a company that works with CBG. The authors call for larger studies.
