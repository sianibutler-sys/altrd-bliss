The law, so far

Updated October 23, 2026
  1. 2018The Farm Bill makes hemp legal: cannabis with no more than 0.3% delta-9 THC by dry weight.
  2. January 2023The FDA says its food and supplement rules don’t fit CBD and asks Congress for a new path.
  3. November 12, 2025Congress narrows the definition of hemp in a funding law, with a one-year runway.
  4. September 2, 2026A new law pushes most of the change back 30 days.
  5. November 12, 2026Cannabinoids made outside the plant lose federal hemp status.
  6. December 11, 2026Finished products with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per container fall outside the definition.
  7. Still in CongressBills to regulate hemp drinks instead, or to delay the change to 2028. All are still in committee.

Say it right

A short glossary
CBD
Cannabidiol. A non-intoxicating compound from hemp, the one most people have heard of.
CBG
Cannabigerol. Another non-intoxicating compound, found in smaller amounts in the plant.
THC
The compound in cannabis that gets you high. Altrd Bliss is made with zero THC.
Hemp
Cannabis that is naturally low in THC, defined by federal law.
COA
Certificate of analysis. A lab report showing what is, and isn’t, in a batch.
Isolate
A single cannabinoid, separated from the rest of the plant.
