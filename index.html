CHECKOUT

In the live build, this button hands your cart to Shopify's secure checkout. Payment runs through your approved hemp processor, the same as today.

  1. Your cart goes to Shopify with every item and quantity.
  2. Shopify checks the shipping state and blocks the ones you can't ship to.
  3. The customer pays on Shopify's checkout page. Card details never touch this site.
  4. Shopify sends the receipt, and the order lands in your Shopify admin.
