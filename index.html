Recipes for your sip

    Questions

    What does it feel like?

    Slow and settled, like sinking into the couch after a long day, while you stay present. CBD eases tension. CBG sharpens focus.

    How do I use it?

    Mix six droppers into sparkling water, tea or a mocktail, or place drops under your tongue for a faster start. Three droppers is a microdose.

    Are there side effects?

    Most people feel none, but everyone responds differently. Talk to your doctor first if you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding.

    Do you ship to my state?

    We ship to most of the U.S. A few states, including Alaska, Hawaii and Washington, D.C., restrict hemp products, and checkout will tell you if yours is one of them.

    How long does a bottle last?

    Twelve months stored somewhere cool and dry. The expiration date is printed on every bottle.

    Can I return it?

    Because these are small-batch food products, we can't take returns once they ship. If your order arrives damaged, leaking or wrong, email hello@altrdbliss.com and we'll make it right.

    Can I sell Altrd Bliss in my store?

    Yes. Email hello@altrdbliss.com to start a wholesale account.
